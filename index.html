My Most Significant Achievement

Turning a stutter into a stage.

TED Talk · High School

I grew up with a stutter that made public speaking feel like my biggest weakness. Instead of avoiding it, I found an unconventional way through it — I learned to sing and freestyle rap, using rhythm as a bridge to fluent, confident speech. I turned that discovery into a TED Talk, not just about the stutter itself, but about a bigger idea: reframing a setback can reveal your actual superpower, especially when the key to unlocking it hides somewhere unexpected, like a melody. Confronting my fear of stuttering in front of others confirmed a belief I now carry into everything I do: I'm capable of doing anything in this world, no matter how daunting the challenge may be.
